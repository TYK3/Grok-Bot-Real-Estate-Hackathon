How to read this page
Evidence is from tonight's hackathon material (live listings, 185 sale brochures, Land
Registry Price Paid matching, and Dwelly's synthetic cases) or from a document we
opened. Assumptions are labelled as such. No market-size, frequency, or willingness-
to-pay figures are invented. Wrong or stale material facts in marketing can breach UK
consumer-protection rules and the National Trading Standards Estate and Letting
Agency Team (NTSELAT) material information guidance that estate and letting agents
are expected to follow. That duty is mentioned only in general terms. This page is not
legal advice and does not cite clause numbers.
Product and customer
Property facts start in many places — the developer, the architect, the sales agency,
the letting agent, the conveyancer, the managing agent, and official registers such as
Land Registry and the EPC register — and they change as a development moves
from planning to sale to management. The same figure is copied from document to
document, often by a different team, and the copies disagree: one brochure says

Entity resolution is the step that makes every later comparison trustworthy. A join is
when the system links a listing or a document to an official record, or two documents
to each other, because they look like the same property. A bad join compares the
wrong properties and raises a false conflict. Tonight's evidence: organiser pre-joins in
the sample were often wrong (Flat 53 Coachman Court was matched to Flat 21), and
a first pass of tenure mismatches was mostly bad joins, not real errors. The rule is
strict: require a postcode and a house number or building name; do not merge on
postcode alone; reject an agent's office address used in place of the property; do
not use organiser pre-joins as ground truth; if the match is not confident, refuse and
hold the item unmatched. Do not invent a conflict from an uncertain join.
Normalisation is what stops measurement artefacts looking like disputes. Store both
square metres and square feet, and always label the basis (NIA, GIA, or another
stated standard). Keep unit, plot and type identifiers as extracted, and map them
only after resolution. Treat freehold, leasehold and share of freehold as a controlled
vocabulary, not free text.
Change propagation
1 A new or revised source arrives
It is ingested through the same pipeline, whether it is a new brochure, a listing edit, or an
official-record refresh.
2 It is attached to the right unit, or held
If entity resolution is not confident, the source is unmatched and no live fact changes.
3 New values are compared with the current fact record
Each field is classified: identical, supersession, measurement or scope difference, or true
conflict.
4 Supersession updates the record
A later document of the same type from the same issuer can replace the value. The
previous value stays in history, with who published it and when.

Roadmap
Contradiction handling
Not every disagreement is a true conflict. The product classifies first, then applies a
rule, then asks a human where the rule is not enough. The voice agent does not
quote a field that is conflicting or pending.
Source-authority hierarchy
Default order, highest first. Recency applies within the same tier. A newer brochure
does not beat a contract. A human must sign off true conflicts, official-record
mismatches, and any override of a higher-authority source. The hierarchy is a
product rule (assumption about what should win), informed by how these documents
are used, not a finding from tonight's data.
1.Signed legal instrument: current title, lease, sale contract, reservation agreement.
Per field: tenure, lease length, ground rent, covenants, pets and subletting, and
the contracted price sit here once signed.
2.Current official register extract: Land Registry title, EPC certificate — always with
the register's date.
Caveat: a Price Paid tenure is as at the sale date and may lag a later private
transaction (Lumley House). An EPC floor area has its own measurement basis.
3.Latest signed specification or measured survey, with the basis stated (NIA, GIA, or
other).
Floor areas, dimensions, specification, finishes, bathrooms, accessibility,
heating and solar as designed or as built.
4.Latest signed price list or formal developer instruction.
Asking price and incentives, before a contract exists.
5.Latest marketing brochure or formal particulars.
Kelso Villa: the October 2026 Carter Jonas particulars sit above the April 2026
particulars for the same issuer and document type.
6.Portal listing, CRM note, or verbal remark.
Lowest tier. Useful as a prompt to check, not as a value to quote. Kelso listings
still at £700,000 are stale against the October brochure.

Voice agent contract
The voice agent is the customer interface, not the product's centre of gravity. It may
only speak from the verified fact record. These rules are the contract between the
fact store and the caller.
1.Answer only from the verified current fact record for a confidently matched unit.
2.Cite the source and its date on every factual answer.
3.State the confidence: verified, pending, conflicting, or unknown.
4.If the state is pending, conflicting, or unknown: do not quote a number or a yes-
or-no as fact; explain briefly; escalate to a human agent; capture the caller's name
and number as a lead.
5.If the unit is not confidently matched, treat the question as unknown. Never
answer from a neighbouring unit.
6.If a listing disagrees with the verified record, quote the verified record and, if it
helps the caller, say the listing looks out of date.
